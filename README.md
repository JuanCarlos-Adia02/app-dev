## My Favorite Series: Pseudo Harem

**Genre:** Romance, Comedy, Slice of Life
**Episodes:** 12
**Episode length:** 23 minutes 52 seconds

### Why I like it
- It has the **best ending**, which is the wedding
- It has a **real ending**, unlike other anime where the characters take forever to confess
- Only 12 episodes, but it has *a lot of character development*
- Rin switches personality types as part of her acting, so it feels like a harem but is really **one person with different personalities**

### Top 3 characters
1. Rin Nanakura
2. Eiji Kitahama
3. Megu

> My favorite romcom because it actually gives the couple an ending.
